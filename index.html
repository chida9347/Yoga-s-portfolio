Project

GREEN UV-VISIBLE SPECTROPHOTOMETRIC METHOD DEVELOPMENT AND VALIDATION FOR ESTIMATION OF EXEMESTANE BY USING DNPH CHROMOGENIC REAGENT

Developed a greener UV-Visible spectrophotometric approach for estimating Exemestane using DNPH chromogenic reagent, with emphasis on practical validation and analytical reliability for pharmaceutical applications.

Problem Statement

Existing estimation approaches can be resource-intensive; a simpler and environmentally considerate UV-based method was needed for Exemestane analysis.

Objective

To develop and validate a green, reproducible spectrophotometric method suitable for routine pharmaceutical estimation of Exemestane.

Tools / Techniques Used

UV-Visible spectrophotometry, DNPH chromogenic reagent chemistry, calibration workflow, and basic validation parameters.

Outcome

Established a workable analytical protocol with consistent response characteristics and a practical validation profile for student-level research and lab use.
